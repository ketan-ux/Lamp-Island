A light-and-shadow diorama

The Lantern Keeper's Shrine

A tiny mossy island in a mist-bound forest pond. In your hand, an old brass lantern carries a living forest spirit. Bring its light close and the grove remembers itself.

Weaving the grove…

In VR

  • Move the lantern close to stones, roots and paper lotuses to wake them.
  • Roll your wrist to tilt the lantern: right pours Spring, left pours Autumn.
  • Cup the flame with your other hand to shield it and turn the season.
  • Trigger / grip / pinch with the empty hand to pass the lantern.
  • Thumbsticks: snap turn (lantern hand), glide (empty hand).

On desktop

  • Mouse look · WASD walk the island.
  • Hold left click to reach the lantern toward what you look at.
  • Q / E or wheel tilt toward Autumn / Spring.
  • Hold right click or Space to shield the flame.
  • M mute · H hints · Esc pause.
